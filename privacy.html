Privacy Policy

How Line MindMap handles browser data, Google account information, and Google Drive content.

Effective date

October 6, 2026

Line MindMap is developed by Muhammad Hamza Mubarak.

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseSetupHelpReport issue
In brief: Line MindMap is a browser-based educational productivity application. Optional Google connection is used to authenticate your account and provide Drive-related features. The application is not designed to sell Google user data or use it for targeted advertising.

1. Information accessed

When you sign in with Google, the app may receive basic profile information such as your account email and profile data supplied by Google. Its Drive-connected features use Google APIs to create, read, update, and organize app-managed files and related images in your Drive.

2. How it is used

Google account information is used for authentication and displaying the connected account. Drive access is used to save, open, update, and organize Line MindMap content and to support supported Drive launch/open actions.

3. Local browser data

The app can store maps and local state in browser storage so browser-only work can persist and cached work can be recovered. Site data stays on the device until cleared or removed.

4. Your Drive

Google Drive-backed maps remain in your Google Drive. The application may use an app-managed “Line MindMap” folder and an “Images” subfolder for organization. The developer does not claim ownership of your Drive content.

5. Sharing

Line MindMap does not sell, rent, or trade Google user data and does not use Google user data for targeted advertising. Support information you voluntarily provide is used to respond to your request and maintain the service.

6. Security

Google OAuth is used instead of asking for your Google password. Access tokens are handled by the browser application and used for Google API requests. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention & deletion

The app does not maintain a developer-controlled database of your mind-map files. Delete Drive-saved files from Google Drive and clear browser-local data using your browser's site-data controls.

8. Third parties

Google authentication and Google Drive are third-party services. Their own terms and privacy practices apply to your use of those services.

9. Educational nature

Line MindMap is an organization tool for medical education. It does not intentionally collect patient records and public support channels should never contain patient-identifiable information.

10. Children

The service is not specifically directed to children under 13 and is not designed for separate advertising or profiling of children.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated when the product or its legal, security, or operational requirements change. Material updates will be reflected by the effective date above.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you voluntarily supplied, use the Report Issue page or the project's official GitHub issue tracker.
