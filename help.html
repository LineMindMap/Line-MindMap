What is Line MindMap?
Line MindMap is a browser-based visual study workspace built for medical students. It combines fast mind mapping, notes, table-based organization, images, keyboard shortcuts, exports, and optional Google Drive storage.
Who is it designed for?
It is designed primarily for medical students across pre-clinical and clinical learning: anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, pathology, pharmacology, microbiology, internal medicine, surgery, pediatrics, obstetrics and gynecology, emergency medicine, radiology, and exam revision.
Is Line MindMap a medical reference or clinical decision tool?
No. Line MindMap is an educational organization and study tool. It does not replace textbooks, peer-reviewed literature, institutional protocols, clinical guidelines, or advice from qualified healthcare professionals.
Can I use it without Google Drive?
Yes. You can continue in browser-only mode. Your work can be stored locally in the browser, while Google-connected mode adds Drive-backed saving and opening.
What happens when I sign in with Google?
Google authentication lets the application identify the account you selected and use the Google APIs needed for its Drive features. Your maps are stored in your Google Drive rather than a developer-controlled database.
Why does Google show a permission screen?
Google shows the permission screen because the application is requesting access to specific Google services. Review the permissions carefully before granting access.
Can Line MindMap read all of my Drive files?
The app is designed around its own Drive-managed content and the files you explicitly open through supported Drive actions. It does not need general access to unrelated Drive files for normal mind-mapping.
What does Auto-save do?
Auto-save continuously saves changes after you make them. In browser-only mode it updates the local copy; with Google Drive connected it also synchronizes changes to Drive when online.
Does Auto-save start enabled?
Yes. The app is configured to start with Auto-save enabled unless you previously disabled it in that browser. You can turn it off from the top toolbar at any time.
How do I open an existing map?
Use File → Open after connecting Google Drive, or open a locally stored map when using browser-only mode.
Can I create separate pages inside one workspace?
Yes. A workspace can contain multiple pages so you can separate subjects or study sessions without creating a separate browser tab for every topic.
Can I use the table mode for medical revision?
Yes. Tables are useful for high-yield comparisons such as drug classes, differentials, organisms, investigations, adverse effects, staging systems, and treatment algorithms.
Can I add images?
Yes. You can add images to mind maps and tables. In Google-connected mode, app-managed images can be stored in the Line MindMap area of your Drive.
Can I export my work?
Mind maps can be exported in several formats supported by the app, including image, text/Markdown, OPML, PDF through the browser print flow, and JSON. Tables include table-specific export options.
Which keyboard shortcuts are available?
Tab creates a child node; Enter creates a sibling; Shift+Tab promotes a node; F2 or double-click edits; Delete removes; slash collapses a branch; Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Shift+Z redoes; Ctrl/Cmd+S saves; Space+drag pans; the mouse wheel zooms.
What if my Drive save fails?
Check your connection, keep working while the browser retains the local state, and try Save again. The status indicator will show when a Drive save is successful or needs attention.
What if I lose access to a map?
First check that the same Google account is connected and that the file still exists in your Drive. Avoid deleting or moving app-managed files until you understand how the Drive location is organized.
How do I delete my data?
Drive-saved maps can be deleted from your Google Drive. Browser-local copies can be removed using your browser's site-data controls. See the Privacy Policy for the data-handling details.
How do I disconnect Google access?
You can stop using the connected account in the app and use Google's account/security controls to review or revoke the application's access.
How do I report a technical issue?
Use the Report Issue page. It creates a pre-filled GitHub issue so the problem reaches the project's official issue tracker with the relevant diagnostic information.
How do I suggest a feature?
Feature requests can be submitted through the Report Issue page by selecting Feature request. Describe the student workflow you are trying to improve and an example of the desired experience.
Should I post patient information in a support ticket?
No. Never include identifiable patient information, private clinical records, passwords, OAuth tokens, private Drive links, or other confidential information in public support issues.
Where can I read the Privacy Policy and Terms?
Use the Legal links in the site header or footer. Those pages describe data handling, Google-connected features, acceptable use, limitations, and your responsibilities.
