Google Drive setup

Use these steps when you want maps to persist across supported devices and sessions through your Google Drive.

01 · Sign in

Open the app and select Sign in with Google. Use the Google account you want to associate with your Line MindMap workspace.

02 · Review permissions

Read Google's permission dialog before continuing. The app requests the services needed to authenticate you and operate its Drive-connected features.

03 · Create your workspace

Start a new mind map, rename it by subject, and use the File menu to save or reopen it later.

Medical-study setup ideas

These structures are tuned for the way medical students actually revise complex material.

Systems & mechanisms

Heart failure → preload/afterload → compensatory mechanisms → symptoms → investigations → management.

Differential diagnosis

Build a central symptom or syndrome and branch into red flags, distinguishing features, tests, and first-line management.

Pharmacology

Organize class → mechanism → indications → contraindications → adverse effects → interactions → monitoring.

OSCE preparation

Structure station prompts, opening steps, focused history, examination, differentials, patient explanation, and closure.

Pathology revision

Cause → molecular changes → morphology → clinical presentation → diagnosis → complications.

Case discussions

Use branches for problem representation, hypotheses, evidence for/against, investigations, and disposition.
