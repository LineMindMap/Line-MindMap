What does Line MindMap do?

Connect related ideas in a mind map, compare topics in a table, and describe sequences and decisions in a flowchart. Notes and images support the existing mind map and table workspaces. Mind maps support local browser storage and optional Google Drive saving. The flowchart workspace saves locally and exports editable JSON, SVG, PNG, and print-to-PDF.

What does “authentic” mean here?

This is the official product profile. It describes the workspace's published features and intended educational use. It does not represent medical accreditation, independent validation, or endorsement of user-created study material.

Educational scope and privacy

Line MindMap organizes information supplied by its users. It does not certify the accuracy of medical notes or replace textbooks, research literature, or clinical guidelines. Private study files and connected Google Drive material are not part of this public profile.
