Line MindMap for Medical Students

Line MindMap is a browser-based study workspace for turning complex medical topics into clear visual maps and structured comparison tables.

Mind mapping

Organize mechanisms, presentations, differentials, investigations, management, complications, and exam-focused revision into visual structures.

Study tables

Create structured comparisons for diseases, drugs, organisms, classifications, investigations, adverse effects, and other high-yield material.

Flexible storage

Use the browser-only workflow or connect Google Drive for app-managed saving and opening.

Launch

Line MindMap officially launched on October 4, 2026. See the official product profile for published product details.

Flowcharts and learning guides

Use the flowchart workspace to organize sequences and decisions, or explore the medical study workflow blogs.

Who it is for

Line MindMap is primarily designed for medical students across pre-clinical and clinical learning, including anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, pathology, pharmacology, microbiology, internal medicine, surgery, pediatrics, obstetrics and gynecology, emergency medicine, radiology, OSCE preparation, case discussions, differential diagnosis, and exam revision.

Product capabilities

  • Mind maps with keyboard-first editing
  • Tables with notes, formatting, and images
  • Rich-text notes and image support
  • Markdown, text, OPML, JSON, image, and browser-print PDF export workflows
  • Browser-only local storage and optional Google Drive storage

Educational scope

Line MindMap is educational productivity software. It is not a medical reference, diagnostic system, clinical decision-support tool, or substitute for clinical guidelines, peer-reviewed literature, institutional protocols, or professional medical advice.
